History About PFC

Establishment: Paro FC began its journey in 2018. First major breakthrough: The club won the Bhutan Premier League in 2019. Building a winning era: After Thimphu City FC won in 2020, Paro FC returned to the top in 2021 and continued winning league titles in 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025. International ambition: The club has competed in Asian Football Confederation (AFC) competitions, representing Bhutan against clubs from other countries. Youth development: Paro FC also invests in football development through its academy and grassroots programmes..
